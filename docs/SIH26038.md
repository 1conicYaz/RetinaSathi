# SIH26038 alignment

## Problem statement

SIH26038 asks for a MATLAB-based, explainable diabetic-retinopathy screening
pipeline for rural India. Its main work packages are image quality and
enhancement, retinal structure and lesion analysis, ICDR Grade 0–4 and
referable-DR screening, clinically meaningful explanation/reporting, and a
Simulink model for district-scale capacity planning.

The expected referable-DR operating point is sensitivity above 90% and
specificity above 85%. The statement also emphasizes variable portable-camera
quality, clinical validation rigor, human review and programs serving more than
100,000 patients annually.

Public reference: <https://sih2026.vuce.in/ps/SIH26038>. The team should compare
this document with the exact SIH portal text at submission time.

## RetinaSathi proposal

1. **MATLAB-integrated AI screening** — frozen V3.4 ONNX inference with matched
   preprocessing, calibration, referral threshold and Grade 0–4 output.
2. **Simulink/SimEvents workflow model** — executable queues and servers for
   capture, recapture, network transfer, inference and clinical review.
3. **Calibrated human-in-the-loop triage** — a dedicated referral head,
   uncertainty routing, protected history and mandatory qualified review.
4. **Safety-aware evidence** — poor inputs stop prediction; model identity and
   decision provenance are retained; unavailable modules are stated explicitly.
5. **Supporting deployment interface** — authenticated InsForge workflow backed
   by a protected Azure Container Apps ONNX service.
6. **Research-to-clinic pathway** — ophthalmologist collaboration, independent
   Indian intended-camera evaluation, validated XAI, edge research and controlled
   pilot work under appropriate governance.

## Presentation boundary

RetinaSathi currently demonstrates research software, source validation and
operational simulation. It does not demonstrate clinical approval, prospective
impact, validated lesion-level explanation or observed district throughput.
