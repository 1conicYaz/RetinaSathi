## Reproducibility

- Seed: `26038`
- Validation fraction: `0.2`
- Exact local manifests: `runs/splits/` (regenerated, never committed)
- Shareable source/manifest hashes and class distributions: `artifacts/split_summary.json`
- IDRiD grading labels do not expose patient IDs; this limitation is explicit rather than guessed from filenames.

## Safety gates

- No official IDRiD test record enters a training or validation manifest.
- All labelled APTOS/IDRiD records resolve to an image.
- DeepDRiD patient sets are disjoint.
- Original files are read only; processed caches must be written outside raw/canonical roots.

## Real-loader smoke evidence

On 2026-09-10, the actual local training roots—not synthetic fixtures—loaded successfully through the versioned dataset classes:

- DeepDRiD quality: 1,200 training and 400 validation images; sample tensor `3×384×384`, binary overall-quality label, and three attribute labels.
- IDRiD lesions: 43 internal-training images; sample tensor `3×1024×1024` with four aligned `1024×1024` masks.
- DRIVE vessels: 16 internal-training images; sample tensor `3×768×768` with one aligned vessel mask.
- IDRiD localization: 330 internal-training images; sample tensor `3×384×384` with four normalized disc/fovea coordinates.

The IDRiD localization release stores training and testing rows together in 515-row CSV files and leaves the opposite partition's coordinate cells blank. The loader explicitly skips fully blank rows, rejects partially populated or duplicate coordinates, and verifies that every selected manifest image has both disc and fovea labels. Both training tables contain 413 valid coordinate pairs, their intersection covers all 413 grading-training images, and unit tests protect this behavior.
